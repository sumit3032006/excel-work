1. What is ERP?
ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning. It is a software system that helps a company manage its main business activities, such as finance, inventory, sales, purchasing, and human resources, in one integrated system.

Example: In a mobile shop, when a customer buys a phone, the ERP system updates the stock, records the sale, and helps maintain the financial records. This helps the shop owner manage business activities efficiently.

Examples of ERP software include SAP, Oracle ERP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and Odoo.
2. What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It is a software system used to manage customer information, enquiries, sales, and communication. It helps businesses maintain good customer relationships and provide better customer service.

Example: When a customer contacts an online store about a laptop order, the CRM system displays the customer's details and purchase history. The support representative can understand the issue, resolve it quickly, and record the interaction for future reference.

Examples of CRM software include Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales.
